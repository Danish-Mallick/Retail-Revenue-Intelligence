More orders.

Why both views? 92.1% is the observed sales share of people who placed multiple invoices at some point. It does not measure whether any particular monthly cohort will return.
The cohort assignment is based on customers' first appearance in this file, not proven first-ever purchase. Month-end truncation and seasonal purchasing can affect retention comparisons.
Scope: Analysis of the supplied 384,721-row, 13-column positive-transaction extract. Mixed-format invoice dates were repaired and derived calendar fields recomputed. Assumed pounds sterling based on the underlying UK-retail context; currency is not stated in the CSV. No profit, cancellations or return-rate analysis is possible.
