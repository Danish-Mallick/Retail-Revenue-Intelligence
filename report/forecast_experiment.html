What the errors mean
WAPE adds up absolute weekly forecast errors and divides by actual units sold during the test. It is not an accuracy percentage, and it does not account for future promotions or inventory restrictions.
The limitation I couldn't model away: this dataset covers about one year of filtered positive sales. It contains no stock availability, unfilled orders, advertising, promotion calendar or reliable multi-year holiday pattern. The quantity sold is a useful signal, but it is not unconstrained customer demand. The 2010 holiday week without transactions is kept as zero observed sales — this does not prove customers wanted nothing.
Reproducibility: scripts/demand_forecast.py generates five non-overlapping, out-of-time four-week validation folds, chooses from three transparent forecasting methods per series, and evaluates one final untouched November–December 2011 four-week period. No data from the final holdout influenced method selection. All displayed numbers are calculated from the aggregated results in this repository.
